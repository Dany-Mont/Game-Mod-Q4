How To Play:
This mod transforms Quake 4 into a third-person survival experience. Fight waves of enemies, survive as long as possible, and track your time survived directly on the HUD. You must stay mobile to outlast your foes. Pick up different weapons along the way to stay alive longer

How to Start:
Launch the Brainrot21 map to start.

Controls:
A / D - Move Left and Right
Left Mouse - Fire Weapon
